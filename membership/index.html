Common Questions

Before You Join.

What happens to my credit if I cancel?⌄

Credit forfeits on cancellation. You keep what you spend before cancel; the remaining balance does not roll over.

When do credits expire?⌄

90 days from issue while your membership is active. The clock resets each period's grant.

Can I pause or downgrade?⌄

Yes. One-click pause from your portal. Downgrade or upgrade at the next billing cycle.

What if I ship fewer than four videos a month?⌄

Member rates always cost less per video than a-la-carte. The catch is using the full credit each month; ship enough to spend it and you come out ahead every time. Ship less and the unused credit goes to waste.

Is there an annual plan?⌄

Not yet. Contact us if you want one.
